About This Package

Siddhi {Most Popular!}: Package

Siddhi is Viraaya Weddings' luxury destination wedding package, carefully put together for couples who want an elevated and expansive celebration with the benefit of venue and vendor management being handled by one experienced team. 

A destination wedding comes with a long list of moving parts. Décor, catering, entertainment, venue coordination, guest hospitality and everything in between each require their own conversations and follow-ups. Handling this independently can easily leave you buried under spam, unread emails and unanswered phone calls.

With Siddhi, the experienced team at Viraaya Weddings takes the effort out of the planning process, handling each element with hands-on oversight from the first ceremony to the last.

With access to some of our most sought-after wedding destinations across India and a curated selection of premium venues capable of hosting celebrations of varying scales, Siddhi is our most well-rounded and flexible wedding event package. You will also be connected with a carefully selected vendor team with proven experience across the full range of Indian wedding celebrations.

As Viraaya Weddings' most popular destination wedding package, Siddhi is a planning partnership that takes the weight off your shoulders, so you and your family can be fully present for every moment.

Why Families Opt for Siddhi

Most families who begin planning a destination wedding independently underestimate how quickly the timelines stretch. Even with an early start, coordinating across venues, vendors and guest logistics takes far longer than expected. Weeks can pass with little to show beyond a growing inbox and a list of unanswered questions.

With Siddhi, that initial effort is taken off your plate. Our luxury destination wedding package gives you access to a curated selection of premium venues and an experienced vendor network, all at pre-negotiated rates. Booking through Viraaya Weddings means you benefit from established relationships and better pricing than you would typically find going directly.
