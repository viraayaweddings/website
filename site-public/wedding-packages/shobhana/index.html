About This Package

Shobhana: Package

Shobhana is Viraaya Weddings' ultimate bespoke destination wedding package, designed for couples who want their venue, vendors and wedding planning handled together by one experienced team.

Planning a destination wedding on your own means approaching venues, decorators, photographers, caterers and entertainment teams separately. Each conversation, negotiation and follow-up takes time, and the list adds up quickly. With Shobhana, Viraaya Weddings' experienced team handles all of it for you. Every element of your wedding is planned and coordinated together, so you can focus on enjoying your special day rather than managing the logistics behind it.

With Shobhana, couples gain access to premium hotels and resorts known for hosting spectacular Indian weddings, alongside transparent pricing and expert guidance from the Viraaya Weddings team. You will also be connected with some of the most experienced wedding vendors in the industry: specialists in décor, photography, entertainment, art, production and more. This will ensure your celebrations are executed beautifully from the welcome festivities to the final farewell.

As a luxury bespoke destination wedding package, Shobhana offers an end-to-end planning experience designed to simplify the journey while elevating every moment of your celebration.
