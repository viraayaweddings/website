OVERVIEW

The Oberoi Rajvilas Jaipur

When you step into a destination wedding at The Oberoi Rajvilas Jaipur, it feels like you're setting foot into a royal palace. The hand-painted frescoes, arched gateways and wide, extensive gardens set the mood for a wedding celebration that looks both rooted in heritage and luxurious. This resort property’s scale appears quite grand, yet every corner echoes with history and a deeply personal charm.

Plan a destination wedding in Jaipur at this 5-star resort, where wedding vows are exchanged under sun-kissed skies, followed by intimate gatherings lit by warm lanterns and soft music. The resort's open rhythm and regal ambience allows each event to feel distinct yet beautifully connected to one another.

What stands out is the signature Oberoi hospitality, paired with world-class service that feels intuitive rather than intrusive, ensuring the celebration feels seamless and smooth, all the while remaining warm, personal and thoughtfully executed.

Frequently Asked Questions

On average, a destination wedding at The Oberoi Rajvilas Jaipur costs somewhere between INR 1 Cr and INR 1.5 Cr for approximately 120 guests. To get a precise estimate on the wedding budget for a 3-day, 2-night celebration, use Viraaya Weddings' Cost Calculator. Enter the logistical information and get an exact quote instantly.

Jaipur International Airport (JAI) is the nearest airport to The Oberoi Rajvilas Jaipur, situated approximately 15 km away. A taxi ride from the airport to the resort takes about 40 minutes.

 

Choose The Oberoi Rajvilas, Jaipur, for an intimate, heritage-led wedding with 71 rooms and easy access, just 15 km from Jaipur airport. However, opt for The Oberoi Amarvilas, Agra, if you want a grander setting with 102 rooms, closer to Agra Airport and iconic Taj-facing views. Both venues fall within a similar INR 1.5-2.25 Cr wedding budget range.

At The Oberoi Rajvilas, Jaipur, standard check-in is 2:00 pm, and check-out is by 12:00 noon. Early check-in and late check-out are available on request, subject to availability.

At The Oberoi Rajvilas Jaipur, alcohol is permitted, but all beverages must be purchased through the hotel. Outside alcohol is not allowed, and drinks are charged separately, as they are usually not included in standard wedding packages.
