How This Consultation Makes a Difference

There’s no shortage of planners, venues and people willing to give advice. But that’s also the problem. Too many opinions, too much conflicting information and no clear direction. At Viraaya Weddings, we simplify all of that.

Cut Out the Middle Man

Getting clear answers shouldn’t take this much effort. You’re switching between venue teams, planners, online directories, and endless recommendations just to piece things together. Friends and family share contacts, but most of them lead you through multiple layers before you reach someone who can actually make decisions.

With Viraaya Weddings, you skip all of that. We connect you directly with hotel decision-makers, not intermediaries, and give you access to a trusted vendor pool.

Access Transparent Pricing

Every single source will claim to give you a final price. Wedding planners, venue teams, specialised vendors, everyone says their number is the one to go with. But because no one is fully upfront, your budget never really feels finalised.

We understand how frustrating that can be. Your budget is one of the most important parts of this process. In this consultation, our expert gives you a clear closing budget based on your vision, backed by negotiated pricing through direct relationships with hospitality partners, so you’re working with numbers you can actually trust.

Secure Expert Support

This is a big day, and it comes with some big decisions. You want it to be perfect, magical, everything you’ve imagined, but you also worry about getting carried away or missing something important.

Our expert gives you an objective, experienced perspective from within the industry. You tell us what you like, what you don’t and what you’re already set on, and we help you understand if you’re making the right choices, both financially and for your guests.

Is This Consultation Right for You?

This consultation works as your starting point, your sounding board, and your reassurance, all in one. If any of these situations feel familiar, we promise that you’ll get real value out of this conversation.

You Don’t Know Where to Start

You know you want a destination wedding, but everything after that feels unclear. Which city makes sense, how do you shortlist venues, when do you start booking vendors and what actually matters first? It’s easy to feel stuck before you even begin. In this consultation, our expert helps you break things down into simple, clear steps so you’re not stumbling your way through the process.

You’re Confused Between What to Pick

You’ve done your research and shortlisted a few venues or destinations, but now everything looks equally good. The more you read, the more confusing it gets, and most information online feels either exaggerated or incomplete. Instead of going in circles, our expert helps you compare your options properly based on your priorities, so you can make a decision that feels right from the start.

You’re Tired of Uncertain Budgets

Every time you ask for a quote, the numbers change. Different planners, different vendors, different estimates, and none of it feels reliable. It becomes difficult to understand what you should actually expect to spend. In this consultation, our expert gives you a clear, realistic budget based on your vision, backed by direct hospitality partnerships. That means you get access to negotiated pricing and a more accurate final closing budget, not just rough estimates.

You Need Some Reassurance

You might already have a strong vision and know exactly what you want. But when it comes to making final decisions, there’s some hesitation. What if there’s a better option or something you’ve missed? That’s where this consultation helps. Our industry expert reviews your plan, validates your choices, and gives you the confidence that everything you’re deciding is aligned with how destination weddings actually work.
