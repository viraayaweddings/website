Jodhpur: The Blue City

Why Choose Jodhpur For Your Destination Wedding

A destination wedding in Jodhpur feels like stepping straight into a fairytale. Forts, havelis and golden dunes turn every ritual into a moment worth remembering, long before the décor is even set.

A City Etched in Royal Legacy

Mehrangarh Fort forms a dramatic backdrop to every celebration, adding a sense of grandeur to baraats and pheras. Meanwhile, Umaid Bhawan Palace offers an elegant setting for everything from intimate mehendis and lively sangeets to lavish wedding receptions.

Wander the blue lanes of Brahmpuri or catch a dune-side sundowner near Osian for pure magic, then unwind at one of Jodhpur's resorts for weddings, where hospitality feels genuinely personal.

Our Wedding Planning Process

How Viraaya Weddings Plans Your Jodhpur Wedding

We understand how important your wedding day is, often the most cherished moment of your life. That is why we never compromise on how we plan and deliver it. Every detail is thoughtfully planned and carefully executed, ensuring your celebration feels immaculate for your guests and genuinely special for you.

Step 01
Initial Consultation & Planning

We begin with a detailed conversation to understand your guest count, room requirements and preferred dates. Every detail is shaped from scratch, with no fixed templates.

Step 02
Venue Shortlisting & Visits

We shortlist 3 to 5 Jodhpur wedding venues as per your brief, arrange an in-person visit and share clear comparisons to help you choose with confidence.

Step 03
Negotiation and Contracting

Multiple venues are shortlisted and compared, but only one is ultimately contracted. Based on your preference, Viraaya Weddings negotiates on your behalf to secure the best price, terms and inclusions.

Step 04
Décor & Vendor Curation

A tailored mood board is created for each function, followed by access to a curated selection of Viraaya Weddings' exclusive circle of trusted florists, caterers, photographers, performers, entertainers and wedding vendors.

Step 05
Guest & Travel Management

From the moment your guests step into their cab to settling into their hotel room, welcome hampers, smooth RSVPs and pre-check-in coordination ensure every arrival feels effortless and warmly attended to.
