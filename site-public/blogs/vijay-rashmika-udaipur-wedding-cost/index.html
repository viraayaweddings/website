Vijay & Rashmika Udaipur Wedding Cost & Venue Guide

August 07, 2026 • Viraaya Weddings Team

Every couple of months, a celebrity wedding takes over our feeds, and this year, it was Vijay Deverakonda and Rashmika Mandanna, who tied the knot at Mementos by ITC Hotels, Ekaaya Udaipur. Within hours of the news breaking, one question was everywhere: how much did the wedding actually cost?

Reports on the Vijay & Rashmika wedding cost have varied wildly, with some pegging it at around INR 12 Cr and others estimating it closer to INR 20 to 30 Cr, once we factor in multiple ceremonies, the reported gold and the grand reception in Hyderabad. The wedding itself took place at a luxury hilltop resort in the Aravalli Hills, where industry estimates suggest that a destination wedding usually ranges from INR 90 lakhs to INR 2.5 Cr, depending on scale and guest count.

At Viraaya Weddings, we get a version of this question almost every week, just without the celebrity gossip attached. “What does a wedding like that actually cost?” is really code for “how do I budget for mine?” So we thought it would be worth pulling apart the reported cost of Vijay & Rashmika's wedding, separating fact from speculation and using it as a real example of how Udaipur weddings are priced.

 

So, What Does a “Normal” Udaipur Wedding Cost?

Here's the part that should put your mind at ease: the wedding cost of Vijay and Rashmika is from a different universe entirely, one with private OTT deals, multiple cities and hundreds of crew members. Your Udaipur wedding cost doesn't have to look anything like that.

Based on what we see across the city's hotels and palaces, here's a reliable cost breakdown for destination weddings in Udaipur in 2026:

The cost of a Udaipur destination wedding can vary widely, but most couples don't need a celebrity-sized budget to create a memorable experience.

 

What Determines the Cost of a Destination Wedding in Udaipur?

Many couples assume the venue is where most of the money goes. While it is often the largest expense, guest count, décor and entertainment, guest logistics and experiences can quickly add up as well.

Venue (per person, per day): INR 15,000 to INR 60,000:

This is your base package, covering accommodation, meals and party areas. Meals are never billed separately from the venue. Heritage palaces sit at the higher end of this range, while modern luxury resorts tend to fall lower to mid-range.

Everything else sits outside this base pricing and package, and it is where most of the cost variation occurs:

  • Décor: INR 30 lakhs to INR 70 lakhs+, depending on scale and design complexity.
  • Stage production: INR 7 lakhs to INR 10 lakhs
  • Entertainment: INR 10 lakhs to INR 20 lakhs
  • Photographer: INR 5 lakhs to 8 INR lakhs
  • All-day lounge: INR 2 lakhs to INR 4 lakhs
  • Room hampers: INR 1 lakh to INR 3 lakhs

Also, this is often the point at which palace wedding costs in Udaipur start to vary significantly. A heritage property and a modern luxury resort can have wildly different per-plate and per-room rates, and that difference alone can shift your total budget by tens of lakhs.

 

Why Couples Keep Choosing Udaipur Anyway

Even with rising budgets, Udaipur remains a favourite choice for destination weddings.

The city offers something genuinely rare: lakes, palaces and centuries of history that photograph beautifully without you needing to do much at all. Wedding venues in Udaipur range from intimate heritage havelis to sprawling five-star palaces on private islands, which means there's a fit for almost every budget and guest list, not just the ones making headlines.

The Vijay & Rashmika wedding cost prompted many couples to take a closer look at the cost of an Udaipur destination wedding. While most people won't have a celebrity-sized budget, Udaipur offers fairytale-like wedding settings across a range of price points. The key is choosing an experience that fits your vision and budget.

Getting Your Udaipur Wedding Budget Right

The conversation around the Vijay & Rashmika wedding cost highlights an important reality: there is no one-size-fits-all budget for a Udaipur wedding. The right budget depends on the celebration you want.

Start by finalising your guest list and preferred wedding season, as both have a major impact on the overall budget. Next, think about the kind of setting you want. A heritage palace and a modern luxury resort can offer very different experiences, and that one decision alone can change your estimated palace wedding cost in Udaipur by several lakhs.
