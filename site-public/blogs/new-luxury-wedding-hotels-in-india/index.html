India’s Newest Luxury Wedding Hotels: A First Look at Leela Jaisalmer, Fairmont Agra & More

August 07, 2026

If you’re planning a destination wedding over the next year or two, you have something previous couples didn’t: a whole new set of luxury hotels to choose from. Between 2026 and 2027, six new five-star properties are opening across India, including three in Rajasthan. Booking early doesn’t just give you more choice. It can also mean better availability, launch offers and room blocks before demand picks up.

At Viraaya Weddings, we track these openings the moment they’re announced, because early access is where the real savings sit. Here’s everything you need to know about the new luxury wedding hotels in India before everyone else catches on.

 

Which New 5-Star Wedding Hotels in India Are Opening Right Now?

With the checklist out of the way, here’s a detailed breakdown of each property, what sets it apart and what it offers for destination weddings:

 

1) The Leela Jaisalmer

Rooms: 80, across villas and tented suites

Residential Guest Capacity: Approximately 160 to 240 guests

Dining: Multiple restaurants and bars

Opening: 15th December 2026

Set across 30 acres of desert landscape near the UNESCO-listed Jaisalmer Fort, this is The Leela’s first entry into Jaisalmer and one of the more striking new wedding venues in Rajasthan. The resort houses a pillarless ballroom and extensive outdoor spaces for weddings and events, which makes it a strong pick if you want desert dunes in your wedding photos without sacrificing five-star service.

 

2) Fairmont Agra

Rooms: 205, including 44 suites

Residential Guest Capacity: Approximately 410 to 610 guests

Dining: 4 venues, a signature restaurant, speciality restaurant, coffee shop and bar

Opening: Late 2026

This is the first Fairmont property in Agra, and it fills a real gap. Featuring regal architecture, built along the banks of the Yamuna, just minutes from the Taj Mahal, it brings a new tier of luxury to destination weddings in Agra. Set to open in late 2026, it’s worth serious consideration for its prime location and authentic design.

 

3) Mayfair, Bhubaneswar

Rooms: 100 (flagship Mayfair Lagoon property)

Residential Guest Capacity: Approximately 200 to 300 guests

Dining: 6 restaurants & bars

Opening: September 2026

Mayfair is expanding its footprint in Bhubaneswar, a city known for its temple architecture. The new property, opening in 2026, positions itself as one of India’s latest luxury wedding resorts for couples looking east instead of west. Its generous scale makes it a great fit for weddings with multiple functions, without having to move guests between venues.

 

4) Kingsbury Lake Island Resort, Udaipur

Rooms: 145

Residential Guest Capacity: Approximately 300 to 440 guests

Dining: Multiple restaurants and bars

Expected Opening: January 2027

Unlike most hotels that adapt existing banquet halls for weddings, Kingsbury was designed from the ground up as an event destination, with rooftop celebration spaces and water-facing views. For a mid-sized wedding in Udaipur, this is one of the more interesting newly launched wedding venues in India to keep an eye on. 

 

5) Conrad Jaipur

Rooms: 246

Expected Guest Capacity: Approximately 250 to 300 guests

Dining: Multiple restaurants and bars

Expected Opening: 2027

This marks Hilton’s second luxury Hilton brand in Jaipur, and it’s built with weddings in mind from the start. If your timeline stretches into 2027, Conrad Jaipur by Hilton can be worth holding a placeholder date for.

 

6) Grand Hyatt Jaipur

Rooms: 409, set across 21 acres

Residential Guest Capacity: Approximately 800 to 1200 guests

Dining: Multiple restaurants, bars and a club lounge

Expected Opening: Late 2026

Grand Hyatt’s Rajasthan debut adds another internationally recognised luxury brand to Jaipur’s wedding scene. If you’re planning a destination wedding in 2026 or beyond, it’s one of the new 5-star wedding hotels in India worth watching as bookings begin.

 

Why Booking in the First Season Actually Works in Your Favour

Newly launched wedding venues in India offer more than just fresh spaces and Instagram-worthy photo spots. Booking early can also give you access to a few advantages that become harder to find later:

  • More flexible pricing, as many properties are still establishing themselves in the wedding market.
  • Better date availability, giving you a wider choice of weekends and celebration dates.
  • Greater room inventory, making it easier to accommodate larger guest lists at one property.
  • A fresh venue, where your wedding won’t look like countless celebrations that have already taken place.
  • Closer coordination, as newer properties often work more closely with early wedding bookings to ensure everything runs smoothly.
