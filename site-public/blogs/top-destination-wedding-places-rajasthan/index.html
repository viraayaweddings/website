Top 10 Destination Wedding Places in Rajasthan for Luxury Celebrations

August 07, 2026 • Viraaya Weddings Team

Every third couple who walks into a conversation with us at Viraaya Weddings mentions Rajasthan before we even ask where they’re thinking of getting married. Lately, it has become the default answer for anyone who wants their wedding to look like it belongs in a history book rather than a banquet hall.

At Viraaya Weddings, we’ve guided families through venue searches across Udaipur, Jaipur, Jodhpur and Jaisalmer, and one thing is clear: Rajasthan has no shortage of stunning wedding venues. The real difficulty arises when you have to narrow ten stunning hotels down to the one that actually fits your guest count, preferred dates and budget. So here’s a list of destination wedding places in Rajasthan that we get asked about most, along with what makes each one worth a closer look.

 

Which Udaipur Palaces Suit a Destination Wedding?

Udaipur is where most couples planning a destination wedding start looking, and it’s usually the city that ends up on the shortlist regardless of where the search began:

  • Taj Lake Palace in Udaipur sits in the heart of Lake Pichola and is best known for an intimate palace wedding in Rajasthan. Its smaller scale is exactly what attracts couples looking for exclusivity over size.
  • The Oberoi Udaivilas offers sweeping lake views, private domed pavilions and a service standard that is hard to match anywhere else in the state.
  • The Leela Palace Udaipur strikes a lovely balance between grandeur and intimacy, with spacious lawns and elegant banquet spaces that comfortably accommodate both mid-sized and larger celebrations.

At these properties, boutique weddings generally start from around INR 50 lakhs, while larger multi-day celebrations can range between INR 2 and 3 Cr, depending on your guest count, catering and overall wedding plans.

If you’re set on a lakeside setting, we’ve put together a closer look at destination wedding venues in Udaipur that compares these options side by side.

 

What Makes Jaipur a Strong Choice for a Royal Wedding?

Jaipur is often the easiest city to choose when guests are flying in from overseas. With an international airport and strong road connectivity, getting everyone there is simpler, without giving up the royal palace setting Rajasthan is known for.

  • Rambagh Palace, once home to the Jaipur royal family, remains one of the most iconic luxury wedding venues in Rajasthan and India.
  • Fairmont Jaipur is considered one of the best wedding venues in Rajasthan, bringing a more contemporary read on Rajputana design, with lawns and ballrooms suited to guest lists running into the hundreds.
  • Samode Palace, just outside the city, offers heritage grandeur without the crowds of central Jaipur.

A palace wedding here usually begins around INR 30 to 50 lakhs for essentials, with premium venues and full guest hospitality pushing the total well past INR 1 crore. Our team has mapped out venue-wise comparisons for a destination wedding in Jaipur if you want to go deeper on this city specifically.

 

Why Do Couples Pick Jodhpur for a Grand Destination Wedding?

For a destination wedding in Rajasthan that leans into scale and drama, Jodhpur is hard to beat:

  • Umaid Bhawan Palace rises above the Jodhpur skyline as one of the world’s largest private residences and remains one of India’s most iconic settings for grand, high-profile weddings.
  • Taj Hari Mahal in Jodhpur, a palace-style five-star hotel rather than a heritage monument, hosts opulent receptions across its sprawling lawns and terraces overlooking the blue city below.
  • Indana Palace Jodhpur captures the charm of a royal palace with carved façades, spacious courtyards and elegant wedding venues.

Weddings at properties of this stature typically start from around INR 1 Cr, reflecting their scale and exclusivity. If you’re planning a destination wedding in Jodhpur, we’ve compared these venues in greater detail to help you choose the right fit.

 

Are There Destination Wedding Venues Beyond the Usual Cities?

Two properties worth considering for a destination wedding outside Rajasthan’s big three cities include:

  • Suryagarh, Jaisalmer offers a complete desert wedding experience, with dune-side dinners, candlelit courtyards and the atmosphere of a restored fortress. Celebrations here typically start around INR 2 crore for intimate weddings and can go considerably higher for larger, multi-day events.
  • The Leela Jaisalmer opens on 15th December, bringing signature luxury, warm hospitality and a fresh destination wedding experience to Rajasthan’s desert landscape.

If you’re planning a wedding between October and March, it’s worth starting your venue search 8 to 12 months ahead. In-demand dates fill up fast, and rates typically rise as availability shrinks. Booking through Viraaya Weddings early also gives you the advantage of our negotiated rates.
